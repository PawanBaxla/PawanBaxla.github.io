Crafting tools that stay out of your way.

Modern mobile software is filled with artificial dependencies: mandatory accounts, recurring subscription paywalls, and pervasive telemetry that sends every tap back to an analytics cluster.

My approach is simple: build self-contained, native Android tools that perform heavy processing right on your phone's processor. No cloud servers to go down, no trackers monitoring your habits, and no recurring fees for offline functions.
