Photo Size Reducer

Compress photos & resize videos. Reduce image size to KB and save storage space.

Are you running out of storage space on your phone? Compress Photo & Video Resizer is the ultimate media optimization tool designed to shrink your files without destroying their visual quality. Whether you are a professional photographer needing to preserve original metadata or an everyday user looking to free up gigabytes of space, this app gives you complete control over your digital gallery.

Unlike basic apps that lock you into generic settings, we offer a Manual-First Dashboard paired with an advanced On-Device AI Assist. You can manually tweak every parameter or let our intelligent algorithm calculate the mathematical sweet spot for maximum file size reduction with zero perceived visual loss.

📸 Advanced Image Compression

Granular Control: Adjust quality percentages and resolution exactly how you want it.
Physical Dimensions: Resize photos by exact physical dimensions (Cm, Inches, Pixels) and set custom DPI for printing or web uploads.

🎥 Pro Video Transcoding

Custom Quality: Shrink 4K videos to 1080p, 720p, or 480p and adjust target bitrate.
Hardware Accelerated: Fast processing utilizing your device's native hardware encoders.
Audio Config: Keep original audio, downsample it, or mute entirely.

🧠 Smart AI Auto-Optimize

Don't want to guess settings? Tap the AI Auto-Optimize button. Our local engine instantly scans media complexity, textures, and edges to apply the absolute best compression settings automatically.

🛡️ 100% EXIF Preservation

Never lose your data. GPS coordinates, camera model, ISO speed, and creation dates are flawlessly copied from the original file to your compressed file. Optimized files inherit custom filenames with timestamps.

🔍 Live Preview

Take the guesswork out of compression! Use our Preview Image feature that shows you exactly what the final result will look like in real-time before you hit save.

🔒 100% Local & Private

Your privacy is our highest priority. All media processing and AI calculations happen entirely offline on your local device. We do not upload, store, or transmit your files to any cloud servers.
